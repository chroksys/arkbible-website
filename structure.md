# 🏛️ Arkbible Website Development Guidelines

This document serves as the official structural and architectural specification for the **Arkbible** companion website.

The primary objective of the website is to **build, nurture, and grow a global, godly Christian community** anchored in Scripture, mutual prayer, and fellowship. The **Arkbible Mobile App** is showcased on a dedicated product page as the official digital tool provided to help community members maintain a quiet, ad-free daily sanctuary.
